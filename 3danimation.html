ABOUT TO 3D ANIMATION

3D animation is the process of creating moving characters, objects, and environments in a three-dimensional space. It is widely used in movies, games, advertisements, architecture, and visual effects. Designers use software like Maya, Blender, and 3ds Max to create realistic models and animations. 3D animation adds depth, lighting, and smooth movement to make scenes look more realistic and attractive. This field requires creativity, modeling, texturing, and animation skills. Many studios and companies use 3D animation for entertainment and professional projects. It is a popular career option in the media, gaming, and film industry.

what does a 3D ANIMATION do

CHARACTER ANIMATION

3D character animation is the process of creating and moving digital characters in a 3D space. It is used in movies, games, and advertisements to make characters look realistic and alive.

3D MODELLING

3D modeling is the process of creating a digital 3D shape or object using software. It is used in 3D animation, games, and movies to design characters, buildings, and objects.

RIGGING & RENDERING

Rigging is creating a skeleton for a 3D model so it can move. Rendering is the final step that produces the finished image or video with lighting and effects.

VFX & VISUAL EFFECT

VFX (Visual Effects) adds digital effects to videos to make scenes more realistic or creative. It is used for things like explosions, magic, and action effects in movies and games.

PRODUCT ANIMATION

Product animation is the process of creating 3D animations to showcase a product in a realistic and attractive way. It is used in marketing, ads, and presentations to highlight features.
