ABOUT GRAPHIC DESIGN

Graphic design is the art of creating visual content creatively. Designers use colors, typography, images, and layouts to communicate ideas. It is used in logos, posters, advertisements, websites, and social media. Good graphic design attracts attention and improves brand identity professionally. Software like Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator are commonly used by designers. Graphic design helps businesses promote products and connect with audiences effectively. It is an important skill in modern digital marketing and media industries.
