ABOUT TO VIDEO EDITING

Video editing is the process of creating and improving videos professionally. It helps to cut, arrange, add effects, music, and transitions in videos. Video editors use software like Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects for editing work. It is widely used in YouTube videos, movies, advertisements, and social media content. Good video editing makes videos more attractive, creative, and engaging for viewers
