ABOUT TO UI UX

UI/UX design is the process of creating user-friendly and attractive digital interfaces for websites and mobile apps. UI stands for User Interface, which focuses on colors, buttons, layouts, and visual design. UX stands for User Experience, which improves how users interact with a product easily and smoothly. Designers use tools like Figma and Adobe XD to create wireframes and prototypes. UI/UX design helps make websites and apps more modern, simple, and engaging. This field requires creativity, problem-solving, and design skills. UI/UX design is an important career option in the technology and digital industry.
