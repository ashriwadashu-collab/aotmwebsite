ABOUT TO 2D ANIMATION

2D animation is a creative process used to make characters and objects move in a two-dimensional space. It is commonly used in cartoons, movies, advertisements, games, and social media content. Animators create scenes by drawing frames and adding motion effects to bring ideas to life. Popular software for 2D animation includes Adobe Animate, Toon Boom, and After Effects. This field requires creativity, drawing skills, and knowledge of animation principles. 2D animation helps businesses and creators present stories in an attractive and engaging way. It is an important career option in the media, entertainment, and design industry.
